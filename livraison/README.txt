DESKQUARIUM v0.13 - pixel art aquarium wallpaper + small idle game (Windows 10/11, 64-bit)

NEW NAME (v0.13)
- "Aquarium" is now called Deskquarium. Download: the Deskquarium-vX.Y zip from
  https://github.com/Sneakyquaza/deskquarium/releases (public).
- Already had it? Nothing to do: it updates itself (your aquarium.exe keeps its name), your
  save moves by itself from %APPDATA%\aquarium to %APPDATA%\Deskquarium, and "Launch when
  Windows starts" carries over.

LANGUAGE
- The game is in English or French. On first launch it follows your Windows language; change it
  any time in Hatchery > Settings > Language (applies immediately).

START
- Unzip the folder anywhere (for example Documents\Deskquarium) and run deskquarium.exe.
- The aquarium is drawn behind your desktop icons, on top of your wallpaper.
- Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" (unsigned exe): "More info" then "Run anyway".

PLAY
- Fish icon next to the clock (notification area, sometimes under the ^ arrow):
  left click = open the Hatchery; right click = menu (Open the Hatchery, Pause, Quit...).
- Shortcut: Ctrl + Alt + A opens the Hatchery.
- Your fish earn money (counter at the bottom left of the main screen), even while you're away
  (up to 8 hours).
- Spend it on eggs in the Hatchery: 8 eggs, 9 rarities (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary,
  Mythic, Celestial, Unreal, Prehistoric), 61 species from the Clownfish to the Kraken, the
  Loch Ness Monster and Jörmungandr. Shiny fish (1 in 100) and modifiers (Gold, Diamond,
  Electric, Rainbow, Shadow, Crystal, Galaxy) earn more.
- NEW in v0.11: every egg has its own animation and its own hatching show (black hole and
  galaxy, solar eclipse, melting amber, eyes in the dark, giant oyster and pearl, whirlpool,
  treasure shower, rainbow prism). The crack light climbs white -> blue -> purple -> gold ->
  rainbow to tell the rarity. Open x3 or x10 at once, Auto open (stops on the rarity you choose
  or a shiny), bad-luck protection (the rarity shown on the egg card is guaranteed after a long
  streak without it), sounds in the Hatchery only (volume and mute in Settings; the wallpaper
  stays silent), big hatches announced on the wallpaper.
- Decor: 6 themes and 14 items to buy and place (Hatchery > Decor).
- Grab a fish: on an EMPTY part of the desktop, hold the left button on a fish for a moment.
  A short click shows its card. Settings > Fish and mouse to turn it off.

UPDATES
- The app updates itself from https://github.com/Sneakyquaza/deskquarium/releases (public).
  Hatchery > Settings > Updates > "Install vX.Y.Z". Nothing is changed if anything goes wrong.

IF THE AQUARIUM DOESN'T SHOW ON THE DESKTOP
- Right click the fish icon > "Embedding test (colours)", then "Other embedding method", or
  "Window mode". "Open the log" shows journal.log.

QUIT / UNINSTALL
- Right click the fish icon > Quit: your normal wallpaper comes back.
- Untick "Launch when Windows starts" in Settings, quit, delete the folder. Your save is in
  %APPDATA%\Deskquarium (delete it to erase everything).
